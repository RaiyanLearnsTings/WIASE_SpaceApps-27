Third-party material inside Z-Anatomy
-------------------------------------
Z-Anatomy states that it used the following as reference and/or included and
adapted them. It does not say which objects they affect:
  - "Brainder" and "White matter" from the University of Washington
  - "Cranial Nerves and Foramina - by University of Dundee, CAHID - CC-BY 4.0"
  - "Anatomy of the Inner Ear - by University of Dundee School of Medicine - CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0"
  - "Kidney - by Lissie Cowley - CC-BY-NC 4.0" (no kidney is used here)
The "ears" (cochlea + vestibule) object may derive from the Dundee inner-ear
model, whose licence is NonCommercial.

Changes made
------------
Selected structures were extracted, joined, re-tubed (curves), decimated,
remeshed (skin), smoothed, rescaled to 1.78 m, re-coloured and exported
as meshopt-compressed glTF. The phrenic nerves are not present in Z-Anatomy;
their paths were approximated (see tools/build_body.py). Full build scripts:
tools/ in the repository that contains this file.
